# Fig6f additional contrast-selected review illustration

Frozen network8, eight10-cm targets, primary0.10/0.10 noise, saved successful visualization attempts only. For each target, the four Intact/Control trajectories with lowest ideal-straight-path RMS and the four Block trajectories with highest RMS are displayed. Every available saved success is considered (Intact30 per target; Block30,30,30,4,29,30,30,30). RMS is the time-RMS shortest distance to the closed center-to-center line segment over movement onset through first entry into the1.5-cm target zone; exact rank ties are resolved by ascending attempt ID.

This deliberately maximizes the four-trial mean pathRMS difference (Block minus Intact) over the available four-trial subsets in each target. It is a contrast-selected illustration, NOT random sampling, a statistically typical example, or an inferential assay. The visual contrast is intentionally exaggerated by extreme selection; its magnitude must not be interpreted as an unbiased condition effect.

Thin lines show the selected native unsmoothed trajectories up to first target-zone entry. Thick curves are arithmetic means of the same four displayed paths after onset-to-entry interpolation onto101 equally spaced movement-phase points (not medians, not all-success means). The exact eight-target manuscript colors,1.5-cm target zones, equal spatial scales and aspect ratios are retained. A common display frame is derived from all453 successful paths and all target zones before applying5% padding.

All quantitative manuscript results use their independently validated datasets and remain unchanged. This additional panel does not replace the current manuscript Fig6f; the five fixed-seed random alternatives and Fig6g/h are preserved.

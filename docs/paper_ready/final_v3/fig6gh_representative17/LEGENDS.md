# Caption-ready definitions

## Fig. 6g

Illustrative hand-speed profiles for network8,target6. Ten spatially stratified random successful trials per condition were sampled once from the first30 saved successes using fixed seeds and peak-position strata, before inspecting their speeds. The same trials appear in h. Thin skyblue(Intact) and vermillion(Block) solid profiles are native unsmoothed speeds, aligned to movement onset and ending at first entry into the1.5-cm peripheral target zone. Thick curves are pointwise medians of these exact ten profiles, with post-entry samples missing rather than zero. A50-ms Gaussian is applied to the full available raw median using truncated/renormalized actual boundaries, followed by display truncation at the last sample with at least5 active trials. This success-conditioned, deliberately chosen network/target illustration is distinct from the unchanged all-trial ten-network manuscript analysis.

## Fig. 6h

Peak-speed positions and trajectories of the same spatially stratified random successful examples as g, network8,target6. Filled circles indicate each displayed trial's native unsmoothed peak-speed position before target entry; solid paths end at first target-zone entry. Both conditions share the same spatial scale and native orientation. Skyblue=Intact; vermillion=Block. The black dashed center-to-center line is the ideal spatial path. The angled overlapping histogram follows empirical Fig1e and uses all30 successful trials per condition: Euclidean distances from the corresponding all30 coordinatewise median peak position,0.5-cm bins; inset scales1cm and2trials. The selected ten illustrate spatial coverage, not an optimal subset or an inferential sample. Target6 was chosen in a previous visual review; no statistically typical target or formal unimodality claim is made. All aggregate speed-matched dispersion statistics remain unchanged and separate.

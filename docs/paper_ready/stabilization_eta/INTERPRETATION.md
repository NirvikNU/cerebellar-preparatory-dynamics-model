# Scientific-review interpretation

The independent saved-output audit passes for all100 network/eta/condition
points. This is a diagnostic result, not selection or acceptance of a new eta.
All medians and SEs below use the ten networks and the frozen whole-network
bootstrap. REPORT.md and source tables contain the complete five-value curves.

## Main outcome

Reducing the shared residual stabilizer does **not** reveal preserved positive
Intact contraction with selectively lost Block contraction under the binding
panel-D definition. Network-level convergence C is negative in both conditions
at every eta (all100 network/eta/condition summaries). At eta1 it is
-0.098670 +/-0.008860 Intact and -0.141322 +/-0.008748 Block; at eta0 it is
-1.326529 +/-0.032020 and -1.513077 +/-0.031742, respectively. Block is more
divergent descriptively, but Intact is not positively contracting either.
No new inferential-test family was introduced.

This is not a failure of the numerical audit. Positive C was the predeclared
contraction criterion, and negative scores are retained unchanged. The measure
compares held-out trial deviations from target-matched reference window means;
it is neither target-mean equilibrium settling nor the manuscript's unavailable
experimental pre-cue analysis. Reference-only PCA95 counts are substantial
(47-104 across all cases) and vary across eta, as mandated by the fixed rule.

## Dynamics, geometry and movement must be considered together

All target equilibria remain locally stable even at eta0 (reused completed
equation/Jacobian audit). Local stability does not guarantee contraction of the
stochastic cloud or arrival at the intended equilibrium within500ms.
From eta1 to0, median normalized GO bias increases from0.0990 to0.7228 Intact
and0.1030 to0.8058 Block. RMS GO-state dispersion increases from1.3498 to2.9804
Intact and1.4113 to3.3073 Block, in the same raw state coordinates.

The achieved geometry also changes: the paired expected-minus-observed alignment
deficit falls from20.9252 +/-1.2465 percentage points at eta1 to9.3927 +/-1.3175
at eta0. Intact PR stays near3.46 while Block PR falls from6.7541 to6.3280.
Control95 alignment K spans5-7, distinct from the much larger target-matched
reference-PCA counts used for panel D. These are not interchangeable PCA rules.

Movement remains finite, all100 cases pass the unchanged preparation bounds,
and no trial has a near-zero peak or missing prediction window. This is not a
claim that every movement is normal. Block boundary-peak counts increase from125
to233 out of2400 trials; multiple-peak counts are699,710,739,758,735 across the
fixed descending eta grid. Intact counts are0,1,1,1,9 for multiple peaks and zero
for boundary peaks. Every flagged trial is retained. Median within-target endpoint
RMS grows from19.75 to26.38mm Intact and28.63 to61.68mm Block; target separation/
scatter falls from7.36 to5.56 and2.99 to1.38, respectively. Endpoint dispersion
is not target error. The predeclared network1 traces are descriptive examples,
not the basis for choosing a regime.

## Prediction is an outcome, not a selector

PCA-ridge R2 falls from0.994680 +/-0.000628 to0.924246 +/-0.006546 Intact,
and from0.965544 +/-0.011583 to0.812652 +/-0.013193 Block. The paired relative
Block reduction is2.9517 +/-1.1721%,3.0569 +/-0.3237%,6.4503 +/-0.8256%,
11.0535 +/-1.4900% and12.9600 +/-1.5761% for eta=1,0.75,0.5,0.25,0.
Even the eta0 reduction remains below the previously reported empirical43.6%
and29.6% descriptive benchmarks. No setting was chosen to approach those values,
and these prediction changes must be reviewed alongside the worsening dynamical,
convergence and movement measures and the changed geometry.

No RRR, new noise, model/geometry retuning, behavioral prediction or further
parameter family was run. No existing result was overwritten or relabeled.
The original eta1 alignment95, panel-e and RRR outputs remain intact and
uncommitted prediction work remains unstaged. Stop for scientific review.

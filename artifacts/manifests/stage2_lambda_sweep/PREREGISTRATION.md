## Full-dimensional control equations
Let r=ReLU(x), r*=ReLU(x*), A=W−I and B=I200. The frozen raw observability Gramian satisfies A′Qraw+Qraw A+C′C=0. Use its source-defined controller view Q=N(Qraw+Qraw′)/(2 trace(Qraw)); this scalar normalization does not modify the frozen asset.
In normalized time s=t/τ, solve the stabilizing continuous-time Riccati equation:
$$
A^\top P_\lambda+P_\lambda A-P_\lambda^2/\lambda+Q=0,\qquad K_\lambda=-P_\lambda/\lambda,\qquad R_\lambda=\lambda I_{200}.
$$
The linear design minimizes the infinite-horizon integral of δx′Qδx+λ||δu||². No restricted actuator, low-rank truncation or excitatory-only feedback is introduced.
The exact nonlinear source convention is:
$$
u_{\mathrm{tonic}}=x^*-h-Wr^*,\quad u_{\mathrm{specific},\lambda}=x^*-h-(W+K_\lambda)r^*,
$$
$$
\tau\dot x=-x+Wr+h+u_{\mathrm{specific},\lambda}+K_\lambda r
=-x+Wr+h+u_{\mathrm{tonic}}+K_\lambda(r-r^*).
$$
Thus x* is a preparation fixed point by construction, including negative coordinates; no rectification of the error vector is substituted for the difference of rectified rates. λ changes feedback and the equivalent source reference input, not the frozen generator or target. At GO remove both specific input and feedback; retain h and the unchanged movement-onset drive. Use frozen C r and the existing arm forward implementation; torque is gated off before GO.

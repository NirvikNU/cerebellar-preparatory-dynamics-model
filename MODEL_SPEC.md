# Motor Preparation Model Specification## Stage 1 status and scopeStage 1 is **ACCEPTED — FROZEN**. The current project model contains the firstten independently generated source-faithful ISNs that passed all predeclaredmovement-fidelity checks for the eight experimental 10-cm targets. Each memberhas its own fixed `W_j`, spontaneous state and baseline drive, calibratedcommon `C_j`, and eight calibrated `x*_{q,j}`. The released Kao realization isan untouched benchmark and is not a member of the project ensemble. Stage 1 has nopreparatory controller, target-specific tonic preparatory input, thalamic orcerebellar circuit, block condition, noise model, adaptation, or target jump.The official source is pinned at commit`40077d2da16e68ab2ab2cff59ec692b97315980b`.## Cortical generatorThe cortical state has 200 threshold-linear units: 160 excitatory and 40inhibitory. Firing rates are`r(t) = max(0, x(t))`.Movement-period dynamics are`tau dx/dt = -x + W r + h + alpha(t) 1`,with cortical time constant 150 ms, internal Euler step 0.2 ms, and savedsampling interval 1 ms. `W` has exact Dale outgoing-column signs and zerodiagonal. The common target-independent movement input has 50-ms rise time,500-ms decay time, and peak amplitude 5 in source units.The frozen baseline drive is`h = x_sp - W max(0, x_sp)`,so the published spontaneous state `x_sp` is a fixed point when the movementinput is absent.## Movement identityThere are eight target-specific optimal movement initial states `x*_{q,j}` ineach accepted 200-dimensional recurrent network. At movement release,`x(0) = x_q*`.Within a member, the recurrent network, baseline drive, movement input,readout, and arm are identical for every movement. Movement identity is thereforecarried by the cortical initial state, not by a target-specific GO signal.## Motor output and plantEach member's fixed rank-2 readout maps excitatory firing rates to shoulder andelbow torques:`m(t) = C r_E(t)`.Those torques drive the exact published planar two-link arm implementation.`C_j` and `x*_{q,j}` were calibrated once by the unchanged source procedure;the arm was never fitted or changed.## Prospective motor potencyFor the published linearized state-error dynamics,`delta_dot = A delta`, with `A = W - I`,and motor error `C delta`, integrated future motor-output error is`J(delta_0) = delta_0' Q delta_0`,where `Q` solves`A'Q + QA + C'C = 0`.Large-eigenvalue directions of `Q` are prospectively potent; small-eigenvaluedirections are prospectively null. This ranking concerns future motorconsequence and is not a neural-variance PCA.## Accepted Stage 1 ensemble and QCCandidate ISNs used a preassigned one-to-one recurrent/calibration seedschedule. A candidate was accepted only if all eight targets passed the hard2-degree angular-error criterion, 0.01-m radius-error criterion, 0.02-mendpoint-error criterion, 0.002 weighted-torque-cost criterion, the sourcemovement-cost stopping criterion, and all architecture/stability/finite-valuechecks. A failed recurrent realization would be discarded without anothercalibration seed.
